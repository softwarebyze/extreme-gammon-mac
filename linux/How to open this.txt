Extreme Gammon for Linux

1. Unzip this folder.
2. Double-click play-extreme-gammon. If your file manager asks, choose Run.
3. First launch may ask for your password so it can install Wine.
4. After that, Extreme Gammon is in your app menu and on the Desktop.

Needs Ubuntu/Debian, Fedora, or Arch, and a desktop.
First launch downloads the official 14-day XG trial from extremegammon.com.
