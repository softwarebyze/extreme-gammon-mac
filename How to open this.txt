Extreme Gammon for Mac

1. Double-click Extreme Gammon.
2. If Mac says it cannot verify the developer: right-click Extreme Gammon, click Open, then Open again.

First launch takes 5–10 minutes and needs internet.
A status file appears on the Desktop while it works.

The first time the game opens, it may ask for a language and a couple of Next clicks.

Extreme Gammon is a 14-day trial. Buy a license at https://www.extremegammon.com/ to keep it.
